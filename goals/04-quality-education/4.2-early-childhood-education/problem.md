## How progress is measured (UN indicators)

<!-- UN:START indicators -->
| Indicator | What it measures | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| 4.2.1 | Proportion of children aged 24-59 months who are developmentally on track in health, learning and psychosocial well-being, by sex | Tier 2 |
| 4.2.2 | Participation rate in organized learning (one year before the official primary entry age), by sex | Tier 1 |

**Tier 1** = the method is agreed and countries regularly produce data. **Tier 2** = the method is agreed, but data are patchy. Tiers are set by the UN expert group (IAEG-SDGs). Full metadata: [UN SDG indicator metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/?Text=&Goal=4&Target=4.2).
