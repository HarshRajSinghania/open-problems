# 4.7 · Sustainable development education

**Goal 4: Quality education** · UN progress 2026: ⚪ Insufficient data · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** By 2030, all learners should gain the knowledge and skills to support sustainable development. That includes learning about sustainable lifestyles, human rights, gender equality, peace and non-violence, global citizenship and respect for cultural diversity.
>
> **Who it is about:** All learners.
>

<!-- UN:START target -->
