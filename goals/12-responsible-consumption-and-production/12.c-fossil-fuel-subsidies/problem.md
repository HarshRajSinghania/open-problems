# 12.c · Fossil fuel subsidies

**Goal 12: Responsible consumption and production** · UN progress 2026: 🔻 Regression · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** Reform wasteful fossil-fuel subsidies that encourage overuse - for example by changing taxes and phasing out harmful subsidies - to reflect their environmental impact. Take full account of developing countries' needs and protect poor and affected communities.
>
> **Who it is about:** Poor and affected communities; developing countries.
>
> <sub>[Edit](../../../data/plain-language.yml) · community-written, not official UN text. Check it against the [UN target](#the-problem-un-target) below.</sub>
<!-- UN:END plain -->

## The problem (UN target)

<!-- UN:START target -->
> **Target 12.c:** Rationalize inefficient fossil-fuel subsidies that encourage wasteful consumption by removing market distortions, in accordance with national circumstances, including by restructuring taxation and phasing out those harmful subsidies, where they exist, to reflect their environmental impacts, taking fully into account the specific needs and conditions of developing countries and minimizing the possible adverse impacts on their development in a manner that protects the poor and the affected communities
<!-- UN:END target -->
