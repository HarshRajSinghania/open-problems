# 14.a · Research capacity and marine technology

**Goal 14: Life below water** · UN progress 2026: 🔻 Regression · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** Increase scientific knowledge, build research capacity and share ocean technology, following agreed international guidelines, to improve ocean health and help marine life support developing countries' development.
>
> **Who it is about:** Developing countries, especially small island developing States and least developed countries.
>

<!-- UN:START target -->
