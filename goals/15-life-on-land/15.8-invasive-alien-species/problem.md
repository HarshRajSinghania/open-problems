# 15.8 · Invasive alien species

**Goal 15: Life on land** · UN progress 2026: 🟢 On track or target met · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** By 2020, act to stop invasive alien species (plants and animals brought from elsewhere) from arriving, greatly reduce their impact on land and water ecosystems, and control or remove the priority ones.
