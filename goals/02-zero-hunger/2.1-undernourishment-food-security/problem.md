**Goal 2: Zero hunger** · UN progress 2026: 🔻 Regression · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)

> **In plain language:** By 2030, end hunger. Everyone should be able to get safe, nutritious and enough food all year round.

**Tier 1** = the method is agreed and countries regularly produce data. **Tier 2** = the method is agreed, but data are patchy. Tiers are set by the UN expert group (IAEG-SDGs). Full metadata: [UN SDG indicator metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/?Text=&Goal=2&Target=2.1).
