# 11.b · Disaster risk management policies

**Goal 11: Sustainable cities and communities** · UN progress 2026: 🟢 On track or target met · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** By 2020, greatly increase the number of cities and settlements with joined-up plans for inclusion, resource efficiency, climate action and disaster resilience. Manage disaster risk at all levels in line with the Sendai Framework (2015-2030).
>
> <sub>[Edit](../../../data/plain-language.yml) · community-written, not official UN text. Check it against the [UN target](#the-problem-un-target) below.</sub>
<!-- UN:END plain -->

## The problem (UN target)

<!-- UN:START target -->
> **Target 11.b:** By 2020, substantially increase the number of cities and human settlements adopting and implementing integrated policies and plans towards inclusion, resource efficiency, mitigation and adaptation to climate change, resilience to disasters, and develop and implement, in line with the Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction 2015-2030, holistic disaster risk management at all levels
<!-- UN:END target -->

## How progress is measured (UN indicators)

<!-- UN:START indicators -->
| Indicator | What it measures | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| 11.b.1 | Number of countries that adopt and implement national disaster risk reduction strategies in line with the Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction 2015-2030 | Tier 1 |
| 11.b.2 | Proportion of local governments that adopt and implement local disaster risk reduction strategies in line with national disaster risk reduction strategies | Tier 2 |

**Tier 1** = the method is agreed and countries regularly produce data. **Tier 2** = the method is agreed, but data are patchy. Tiers are set by the UN expert group (IAEG-SDGs). Full metadata: [UN SDG indicator metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/?Text=&Goal=11&Target=11.b).
