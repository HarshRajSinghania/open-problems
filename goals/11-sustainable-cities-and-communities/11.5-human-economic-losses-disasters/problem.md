# 11.5 · Human and economic losses to disasters

**Goal 11: Sustainable cities and communities** · UN progress 2026: 🟠 Marginal progress, significant acceleration needed · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** By 2030, greatly reduce the number of deaths and people affected by disasters, including water-related disasters, and the money lost to them compared with the size of the world economy.
>
> **Who it is about:** Everyone, with a focus on poor people and people in vulnerable situations.
>
> <sub>[Edit](../../../data/plain-language.yml) · community-written, not official UN text. Check it against the [UN target](#the-problem-un-target) below.</sub>
<!-- UN:END plain -->

## The problem (UN target)

<!-- UN:START target -->
> **Target 11.5:** By 2030, significantly reduce the number of deaths and the number of people affected and substantially decrease the direct economic losses relative to global gross domestic product caused by disasters, including water-related disasters, with a focus on protecting the poor and people in vulnerable situations
<!-- UN:END target -->

## How progress is measured (UN indicators)

<!-- UN:START indicators -->
| Indicator | What it measures | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| 11.5.1 | Number of deaths, missing persons and directly affected persons attributed to disasters per 100,000 population | Tier 1 |
| 11.5.2 | Direct economic loss attributed to disasters in relation to global gross domestic product (GDP) | Tier 1 |
| 11.5.3 | (a) Damage to critical infrastructure and (b) number of disruptions to basic services, attributed to disasters | Tier 1 |

**Tier 1** = the method is agreed and countries regularly produce data. **Tier 2** = the method is agreed, but data are patchy. Tiers are set by the UN expert group (IAEG-SDGs). Full metadata: [UN SDG indicator metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/?Text=&Goal=11&Target=11.5).
