## How progress is measured (UN indicators)

<!-- UN:START indicators -->
| Indicator | What it measures | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| 11.6.1 | Proportion of municipal solid waste collected and managed in controlled facilities out of total municipal waste generated, by cities | Tier 1 |
| 11.6.2 | Annual mean levels of fine particulate matter (e.g. PM2.5 and PM10) in cities (population weighted) | Tier 1 |

**Tier 1** = the method is agreed and countries regularly produce data. **Tier 2** = the method is agreed, but data are patchy. Tiers are set by the UN expert group (IAEG-SDGs). Full metadata: [UN SDG indicator metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/?Text=&Goal=11&Target=11.6).
