# 3.1 · Maternal mortality

**Goal 3: Good health and well-being** · UN progress 2026: 🟠 Marginal progress, significant acceleration needed · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** By 2030, bring the number of women who die from pregnancy or childbirth below 70 for every 100,000 babies born alive, worldwide.
>
> **Who it is about:** Pregnant women and new mothers.
