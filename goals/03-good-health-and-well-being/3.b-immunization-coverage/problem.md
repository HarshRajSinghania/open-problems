# 3.b · Immunization coverage

**Goal 3: Good health and well-being** · UN progress 2026: 🟡 Moderate progress, acceleration needed · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** Support research on vaccines and medicines for diseases that mainly affect developing countries, and give people access to affordable medicines and vaccines. Developing countries may fully use the flexibilities in global patent rules to protect public health.
>
> **Who it is about:** People in developing countries.
>
> <sub>[Edit](../../../data/plain-language.yml) · community-written, not official UN text. Check it against the [UN target](#the-problem-un-target) below.</sub>
<!-- UN:END plain -->

## The problem (UN target)

<!-- UN:START target -->
> **Target 3.b:** Support the research and development of vaccines and medicines for the communicable and non-communicable diseases that primarily affect developing countries, provide access to affordable essential medicines and vaccines, in accordance with the Doha Declaration on the TRIPS Agreement and Public Health, which affirms the right of developing countries to use to the full the provisions in the Agreement on Trade-Related Aspects of Intellectual Property Rights regarding flexibilities to protect public health, and, in particular, provide access to medicines for all
<!-- UN:END target -->

## How progress is measured (UN indicators)

<!-- UN:START indicators -->
| Indicator | What it measures | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| 3.b.1 | Proportion of the target population covered by all vaccines included in their national programme | Tier 1 |
| 3.b.2 | Total net official development assistance to medical research and basic health sectors | Tier 1 |
| 3.b.3 | Health product access index | Tier 2 |

**Tier 1** = the method is agreed and countries regularly produce data. **Tier 2** = the method is agreed, but data are patchy. Tiers are set by the UN expert group (IAEG-SDGs). Full metadata: [UN SDG indicator metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/?Text=&Goal=3&Target=3.b).
