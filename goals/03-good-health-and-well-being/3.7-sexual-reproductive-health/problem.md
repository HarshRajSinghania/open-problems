# 3.7 · Sexual and reproductive health

**Goal 3: Good health and well-being** · UN progress 2026: 🟠 Marginal progress, significant acceleration needed · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** By 2030, everyone should be able to get sexual and reproductive health care, including family planning, information and education. Countries should build reproductive health into their national plans.

## How progress is measured (UN indicators)

<!-- UN:START indicators -->
| Indicator | What it measures | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| 3.7.1 | Proportion of women of reproductive age (aged 15-49 years) who have their need for family planning satisfied with modern methods | Tier 1 |
| 3.7.2 | Adolescent birth rate (aged 10-14 years; aged 15-19 years) per 1,000 women in that age group | Tier 1 |

**Tier 1** = the method is agreed and countries regularly produce data. **Tier 2** = the method is agreed, but data are patchy. Tiers are set by the UN expert group (IAEG-SDGs). Full metadata: [UN SDG indicator metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/?Text=&Goal=3&Target=3.7).
