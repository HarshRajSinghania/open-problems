# 3.4 · NCD and mental health

**Goal 3: Good health and well-being** · UN progress 2026: 🟠 Marginal progress, significant acceleration needed · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** By 2030, cut by one third the number of people who die too early from non-communicable diseases (illnesses that do not spread from person to person), through prevention and treatment. Support mental health and well-being.
