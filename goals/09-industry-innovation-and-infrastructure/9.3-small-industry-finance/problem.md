## How progress is measured (UN indicators)

<!-- UN:START indicators -->
| Indicator | What it measures | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| 9.3.1 | Proportion of small-scale industries in total industry value added, based on (a) international classification and (b) national classifications | Tier 2 |
| 9.3.2 | Proportion of small-scale industries with a loan or line of credit | Tier 1 |
