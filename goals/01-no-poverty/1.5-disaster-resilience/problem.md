# 1.5 · Resilience to disasters

**Goal 1: No poverty** · UN progress 2026: 🟡 Moderate progress, acceleration needed · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** By 2030, help poor and vulnerable people cope with, and be less exposed to, climate-related extreme events and other economic, social and environmental shocks and disasters.
>
> **Who it is about:** Poor people and people in vulnerable situations.
>

## The problem (UN target)

<!-- UN:START target -->
> **Target 1.5:** By 2030, build the resilience of the poor and those in vulnerable situations and reduce their exposure and vulnerability to climate-related extreme events and other economic, social and environmental shocks and disasters
<!-- UN:END target -->

## How progress is measured (UN indicators)

<!-- UN:START indicators -->
| Indicator | What it measures | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| 1.5.1 | Number of deaths, missing persons and directly affected persons attributed to disasters per 100,000 population | Tier 1 |
| 1.5.2 | Direct economic loss attributed to disasters in relation to global gross domestic product (GDP) | Tier 1 |
| 1.5.3 | Number of countries that adopt and implement national disaster risk reduction strategies in line with the Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction 2015-2030 | Tier 1 |
| 1.5.4 | Proportion of local governments that adopt and implement local disaster risk reduction strategies in line with national disaster risk reduction strategies | Tier 2 |

**Tier 1** = the method is agreed and countries regularly produce data. **Tier 2** = the method is agreed, but data are patchy. Tiers are set by the UN expert group (IAEG-SDGs). Full metadata: [UN SDG indicator metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/?Text=&Goal=1&Target=1.5).
