## The problem (UN target)

## How progress is measured (UN indicators)

<!-- UN:START indicators -->
| Indicator | What it measures | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| 1.2.1 | Proportion of population living below the national poverty line, by sex and age | Tier 1 |
| 1.2.2 | Proportion of men, women and children of all ages living in poverty in all its dimensions according to national definitions | Tier 2 |

**Tier 1** = the method is agreed and countries regularly produce data. **Tier 2** = the method is agreed, but data are patchy. Tiers are set by the UN expert group (IAEG-SDGs). Full metadata: [UN SDG indicator metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/?Text=&Goal=1&Target=1.2).
