# 1.2 · National poverty

**Goal 1: No poverty** · UN progress 2026: 🟠 Marginal progress, significant acceleration needed · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** By 2030, cut by at least half the share of people living in poverty, using each country's own definition of what poverty means there.
>
> **Who it is about:** Men, women and children of all ages.
>

## The problem (UN target)

<!-- UN:START target -->
> **Target 1.2:** By 2030, reduce at least by half the proportion of men, women and children of all ages living in poverty in all its dimensions according to national definitions
<!-- UN:END target -->

## How progress is measured (UN indicators)

<!-- UN:START indicators -->
| Indicator | What it measures | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| 1.2.1 | Proportion of population living below the national poverty line, by sex and age | Tier 1 |
| 1.2.2 | Proportion of men, women and children of all ages living in poverty in all its dimensions according to national definitions | Tier 2 |

**Tier 1** = the method is agreed and countries regularly produce data. **Tier 2** = the method is agreed, but data are patchy. Tiers are set by the UN expert group (IAEG-SDGs). Full metadata: [UN SDG indicator metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/?Text=&Goal=1&Target=1.2).
