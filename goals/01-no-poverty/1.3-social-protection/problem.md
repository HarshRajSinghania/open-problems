# 1.3 · Social protection

**Goal 1: No poverty** · UN progress 2026: 🟠 Marginal progress, significant acceleration needed · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** Every country should set up safety nets - such as pensions, child benefits or unemployment support - that suit its situation. By 2030 most poor and vulnerable people should be covered.
>
> **Who it is about:** Poor and vulnerable people.
>

## How progress is measured (UN indicators)

<!-- UN:START indicators -->
| Indicator | What it measures | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| 1.3.1 | Proportion of population covered by social protection floors/systems, by sex, distinguishing children, unemployed persons, older persons, persons with disabilities, pregnant women, newborns, work-injury victims and the poor and the vulnerable | Tier 1 |

**Tier 1** = the method is agreed and countries regularly produce data. **Tier 2** = the method is agreed, but data are patchy. Tiers are set by the UN expert group (IAEG-SDGs). Full metadata: [UN SDG indicator metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/?Text=&Goal=1&Target=1.3).
