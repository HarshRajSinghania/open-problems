# 1.4 · Access to basic services

**Goal 1: No poverty** · UN progress 2026: 🟡 Moderate progress, acceleration needed · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** By 2030, everyone should have equal rights to money and resources and access to basic services. That includes owning land and property, inheriting, using natural resources and new technology, and using banks and small loans.
>
> **Who it is about:** All men and women, especially poor and vulnerable people.
>
> <sub>[Edit](../../../data/plain-language.yml) · community-written, not official UN text. Check it against the [UN target](#the-problem-un-target) below.</sub>
<!-- UN:END plain -->

## The problem (UN target)

<!-- UN:START target -->
> **Target 1.4:** By 2030, ensure that all men and women, in particular the poor and the vulnerable, have equal rights to economic resources, as well as access to basic services, ownership and control over land and other forms of property, inheritance, natural resources, appropriate new technology and financial services, including microfinance
<!-- UN:END target -->

## How progress is measured (UN indicators)

<!-- UN:START indicators -->
| Indicator | What it measures | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| 1.4.1 | Proportion of population living in households with access to basic services | Tier 1 |
| 1.4.2 | Proportion of total adult population with secure tenure rights to land, (a) with legally recognized documentation, and (b) who perceive their rights to land as secure, by sex and type of tenure | Tier 2 |

**Tier 1** = the method is agreed and countries regularly produce data. **Tier 2** = the method is agreed, but data are patchy. Tiers are set by the UN expert group (IAEG-SDGs). Full metadata: [UN SDG indicator metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/?Text=&Goal=1&Target=1.4).
