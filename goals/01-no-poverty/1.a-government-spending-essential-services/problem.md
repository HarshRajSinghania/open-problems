# 1.a · Government spending on essential services

**Goal 1: No poverty** · UN progress 2026: 🔻 Regression · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** Raise much more money, from many sources and through more cooperation between countries, so developing countries have reliable funding for programmes that end poverty.
>
> **Who it is about:** Developing countries, especially the least developed countries.
>
> <sub>[Edit](../../../data/plain-language.yml) · community-written, not official UN text. Check it against the [UN target](#the-problem-un-target) below.</sub>

## The problem (UN target)

<!-- UN:START target -->
> **Target 1.a:** Ensure significant mobilization of resources from a variety of sources, including through enhanced development cooperation, in order to provide adequate and predictable means for developing countries, in particular least developed countries, to implement programmes and policies to end poverty in all its dimensions
<!-- UN:END target -->

## How progress is measured (UN indicators)

<!-- UN:START indicators -->
| Indicator | What it measures | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| 1.a.1 | Total official development assistance grants that focus on poverty reduction as a share of the recipient country’s gross national income | Tier 1 |
| 1.a.2 | Proportion of total government spending on essential services (education, health and social protection) | Tier 2 |

**Tier 1** = the method is agreed and countries regularly produce data. **Tier 2** = the method is agreed, but data are patchy. Tiers are set by the UN expert group (IAEG-SDGs). Full metadata: [UN SDG indicator metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/?Text=&Goal=1&Target=1.a).
