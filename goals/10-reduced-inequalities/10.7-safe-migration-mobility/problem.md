## How progress is measured (UN indicators)

<!-- UN:START indicators -->
| Indicator | What it measures | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| 10.7.1 | Recruitment cost borne by employee as a proportion of monthly income earned in country of destination | Tier 2 |
| 10.7.2 | Proportion of countries with migration policies that facilitate orderly, safe, regular and responsible migration and mobility of people | Tier 1 |
| 10.7.3 | Number of people who died or disappeared in the process of migration towards an international destination | Tier 1 |
| 10.7.4 | Proportion of the population who are refugees, by country of origin | Tier 1 |

**Tier 1** = the method is agreed and countries regularly produce data. **Tier 2** = the method is agreed, but data are patchy. Tiers are set by the UN expert group (IAEG-SDGs). Full metadata: [UN SDG indicator metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/?Text=&Goal=10&Target=10.7).
