# 10.a · Special and differential treatment (WTO)

**Goal 10: Reduced inequalities** · UN progress 2026: 🔻 Regression · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** Apply the World Trade Organization principle that developing countries, especially the least developed, get special and different (more flexible) treatment in trade rules.
>
> **Who it is about:** Developing countries, especially least developed countries.
