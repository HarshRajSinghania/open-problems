# 5.a · Equal economic rights for women

**Goal 5: Gender equality** · UN progress 2026: ⚪ Insufficient data · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** Change laws and systems so women have equal rights to economic resources, and can own and control land and property, use financial services, inherit and use natural resources, in line with national laws.
>
> **Who it is about:** Women.
>

## The problem (UN target)

<!-- UN:START target -->
> **Target 5.a:** Undertake reforms to give women equal rights to economic resources, as well as access to ownership and control over land and other forms of property, financial services, inheritance and natural resources, in accordance with national laws
<!-- UN:END target -->

## How progress is measured (UN indicators)

<!-- UN:START indicators -->
| Indicator | What it measures | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| 5.a.1 | (a) Proportion of total agricultural population with ownership or secure rights over agricultural land, by sex; and (b) share of women among owners or rights-bearers of agricultural land, by type of tenure | Tier 2 |
| 5.a.2 | Proportion of countries where the legal framework (including customary law) guarantees women’s equal rights to land ownership and/or control | Tier 2 |

**Tier 1** = the method is agreed and countries regularly produce data. **Tier 2** = the method is agreed, but data are patchy. Tiers are set by the UN expert group (IAEG-SDGs). Full metadata: [UN SDG indicator metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/?Text=&Goal=5&Target=5.a).
