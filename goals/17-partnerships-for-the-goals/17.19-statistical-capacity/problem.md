# 17.19 · Statistical capacity

**Goal 17: Partnerships for the Goals** · UN progress 2026: 🟡 Moderate progress, acceleration needed · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** By 2030, build on existing work to create measures of progress on sustainable development that go beyond GDP, and help developing countries build up their statistics.
>
> **Who it is about:** Developing countries.

## How progress is measured (UN indicators)

<!-- UN:START indicators -->
| Indicator | What it measures | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| 17.19.1 | Dollar value of all resources made available to strengthen statistical capacity in developing countries | Tier 1 |
| 17.19.2 | Proportion of countries that (a) have conducted at least one population and housing census in the last 10 years; and (b) have achieved 100 per cent birth registration and 80 per cent death registration | Tier 1 |

**Tier 1** = the method is agreed and countries regularly produce data. **Tier 2** = the method is agreed, but data are patchy. Tiers are set by the UN expert group (IAEG-SDGs). Full metadata: [UN SDG indicator metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/?Text=&Goal=17&Target=17.19).
