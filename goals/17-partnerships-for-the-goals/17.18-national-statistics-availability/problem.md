# 17.18 · National statistics availability

**Goal 17: Partnerships for the Goals** · UN progress 2026: 🟠 Marginal progress, significant acceleration needed · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** By 2020, give developing countries more help to greatly increase good-quality, timely and reliable data, broken down by income, gender, age, race, ethnicity, migration status, disability, location and other relevant characteristics.
>
> **Who it is about:** Developing countries, especially least developed countries and small island developing States.
>

## The problem (UN target)

<!-- UN:START target -->
> **Target 17.18:** By 2020, enhance capacity-building support to developing countries, including for least developed countries and small island developing States, to increase significantly the availability of high-quality, timely and reliable data disaggregated by income, gender, age, race, ethnicity, migratory status, disability, geographic location and other characteristics relevant in national contexts
<!-- UN:END target -->

## How progress is measured (UN indicators)

<!-- UN:START indicators -->
| Indicator | What it measures | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| 17.18.1 | Statistical capacity indicators | Tier 1 |
| 17.18.2 | Number of countries that have national statistical legislation that complies with the Fundamental Principles of Official Statistics | Tier 1 |
| 17.18.3 | Number of countries with a national statistical plan that is fully funded and under implementation, by source of funding | Tier 1 |

**Tier 1** = the method is agreed and countries regularly produce data. **Tier 2** = the method is agreed, but data are patchy. Tiers are set by the UN expert group (IAEG-SDGs). Full metadata: [UN SDG indicator metadata](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/?Text=&Goal=17&Target=17.18).
