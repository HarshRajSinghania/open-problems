# 17.1 · Tax and other revenue collection

**Goal 17: Partnerships for the Goals** · UN progress 2026: 🔴 Stagnation · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** Help countries raise more of their own money, for example through taxes, including with international support so developing countries get better at collecting tax and other revenue.
>
> **Who it is about:** Developing countries.
