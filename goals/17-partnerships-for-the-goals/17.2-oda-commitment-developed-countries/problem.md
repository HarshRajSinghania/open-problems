# 17.2 · ODA commitment by developed countries

**Goal 17: Partnerships for the Goals** · UN progress 2026: 🔴 Stagnation · [All targets in this goal](../README.md)
<!-- UN:END header -->

<!-- UN:START plain -->
> **In plain language:** Developed countries should fully keep their promises on development aid. Many have promised 0.7 per cent of their national income (GNI) as aid to developing countries, and 0.15-0.20 per cent to the least developed countries. Donors are encouraged to aim for at least 0.20 per cent to the least developed countries.
>
> **Who it is about:** Developing countries, especially least developed countries.
>
> <sub>[Edit](../../../data/plain-language.yml) · community-written, not official UN text. Check it against the [UN target](#the-problem-un-target) below.</sub>
<!-- UN:END plain -->

## The problem (UN target)

<!-- UN:START target -->
> **Target 17.2:** Developed countries to implement fully their official development assistance commitments, including the commitment by many developed countries to achieve the target of 0.7 per cent of gross national income for official development assistance (ODA/GNI) to developing countries and 0.15 to 0.20 per cent of ODA/GNI to least developed countries; ODA providers are encouraged to consider setting a target to provide at least 0.20 per cent of ODA/GNI to least developed countries
<!-- UN:END target -->
