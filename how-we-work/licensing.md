| UN goal, target and indicator text; UN report quotations | United Nations terms of use, used with attribution | see below |

## Why two licences?

Apache-2.0 is a widely used, permissive code licence with an explicit patent
grant, which makes it easy for governments, NGOs and companies to adopt and
run solutions. CC BY 4.0 is the standard open licence for written work and
only asks for credit.

## UN content

- Goal, target and indicator wording and tier classifications come from the
  [UN SDG Global Database](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/), United Nations
  Statistics Division. We keep a dated snapshot in
  [`data/un-sdg-framework.json`](../data/un-sdg-framework.json).
- Progress assessments and key messages are quoted from *The Sustainable
  Development Goals Report 2026* with page numbers; see
  [`data/sdg-report-2026.json`](../data/sdg-report-2026.json).
- UN content stays under the
  [United Nations terms of use](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/terms-of-use)
  and is not relicensed by us.
- We do **not** use the UN emblem, the SDG logo, the colour wheel or the 17
  goal icons; their use is restricted by the UN. We only use the goal
  colours as plain colours.
- This is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the
  United Nations.

## Third-party data in solutions

Before using a dataset, record its licence in the problem's "Data we can
use" table. Do not commit data whose licence forbids redistribution;
link to it and write a download script instead.
